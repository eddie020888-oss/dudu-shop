嘟嘟小店 · 帮助

住在灵动岛里的懒懒小宠物，和它一起开的小店。

灵动岛里看不到嘟嘟？

  • 灵动岛需要 iPhone 14 Pro 或更新的机型；其他机型在锁屏看到它。
  • 打开「设置 > 嘟嘟小店」，确认「实时活动」已开启。
  • 打开一次 App，嘟嘟会重新住进灵动岛。

嘟嘟为什么不跟着我变？

  • 在「设置 > 嘟嘟小店」里打开「运动与健身」和「后台 App 刷新」。
  • App 在后台时，系统大约每 15 分钟到 1 小时叫醒它一次，所以变化会慢一点。

步数不对？

在「健康」App 里确认允许嘟嘟小店读取步数。

换手机了，进度还在吗？

用 iCloud 或电脑备份恢复新手机，进度会一起带过去。删掉 App 会清空进度。

Dudu Shop · Support

A very lazy little pet in your Dynamic Island — and the shop you run together.

Can't see Dudu in the Dynamic Island?

  • The Dynamic Island needs iPhone 14 Pro or newer; on other models Dudu appears on the Lock Screen.
  • Open Settings > Dudu Shop and make sure Live Activities is on.
  • Open the app once and Dudu moves back into the island.

Dudu isn't following what I do?

  • In Settings > Dudu Shop, turn on Motion & Fitness and Background App Refresh.
  • In the background, iOS wakes the app roughly every 15 minutes to an hour, so changes can lag a little.

Step count looks wrong?

In the Health app, check that Dudu Shop is allowed to read steps.

New phone — will my progress come along?

Restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup and your progress comes with it. Deleting the app erases progress.
