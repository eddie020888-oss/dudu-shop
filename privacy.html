嘟嘟小店 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

嘟嘟小店是一个和住在灵动岛里的宠物嘟嘟一起开店的小游戏。我们的设计很简单：不联网，不收集。

我们不收集任何个人信息

本 App 不需要注册或登录，不包含广告，不使用任何第三方统计或追踪工具，也不会把任何数据发送到我们或任何第三方的服务器。

存在你手机里的数据

宠物的状态、游戏进度、买到的物品、你设置的作息时间、与嘟嘟的聊天记录，只保存在你的设备上。删除 App 即可全部删除。如果你开启了 iCloud 或电脑备份，这些数据可能随系统备份一起保存，由 Apple 管理。

权限说明（都可选，所有处理都在手机上完成）

  • 健康（只读）：读取当天步数和运动记录。不写入任何健康数据，不用于广告，不提供给任何人。
  • 运动与健身：判断你在走路、跑步还是坐车，让灵动岛里的嘟嘟跟着变。
  • 前置摄像头：“对视”功能在 App 打开时判断你是否在看屏幕。不拍照、不录像、不保存任何画面。
  • 通知：嘟嘟饿了或店里有事时提醒你，全部静音。

你可以随时在系统「设置」中关闭这些权限。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果以后加入需要联网的功能，我们会先更新本政策并在 App 内告知。

Dudu Shop · Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Dudu Shop is a small shop game you run with Dudu, a pet who lives in your Dynamic Island. It's built simply: no internet connection, no data collection.

We collect no personal information

No sign-up or login, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking, and no data is ever sent to us or anyone else.

Data stored on your phone

Your pet's state, game progress, items, the daily schedule you set and your chats with Dudu are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, this data may be included in your system backup, managed by Apple.

Permissions (all optional, all processed on your phone)

  • Health (read only): today's steps and workouts. We never write health data, never use it for ads and never share it.
  • Motion & Fitness: tells whether you're walking, running or in a car, so Dudu in the Dynamic Island can move along with you.
  • Front camera: the “eye contact” feature checks whether you're looking at the screen while the app is open. No photos, no video, nothing saved.
  • Notifications: quiet reminders when Dudu is hungry or something in the shop is ready.

You can turn these off any time in Settings.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever add a feature that needs the internet, we'll update this policy first and let you know in the app.
